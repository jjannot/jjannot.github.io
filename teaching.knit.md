* **Principles of Biology** - Introductory majors laboratory course in organismal biology.


<h2> Illinois State University </h2>
* **Research Program Coordinator** - Annually lead a group of undergraduate students in ecological research, methods, writing, publishing, and scientific career development.

* **Ecology** - Teach the laboratory/field portion of the upper level Ecology course for biology majors. 


<h2> Rocky Mountain Biological Laboratory </h2>
* **Independent Study Program Coordinator** - Mentor and teach undergraduate students from a wide range of research advisors, on scientific research methods, writing, and careers. 


<h2> Montana State University </h2>
